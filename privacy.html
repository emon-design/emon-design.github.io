Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Deskwork handles information when you use the Deskwork website and its browser-based tools.

1. Information you provide

Deskwork does not require you to create an account to use its core browser-based tools.

If you voluntarily contact Deskwork, the information you include in your message may be used to respond to your request.

2. Browser-based tools and files

Some Deskwork tools are designed to process files and information directly in your browser. When a tool performs processing locally, the relevant data can remain on your device and is not uploaded to a Deskwork server for that processing.

The exact behavior can vary between tools. Users should review the information shown on an individual tool before processing sensitive or important documents.

3. Information automatically provided by your browser

Like most websites, the hosting infrastructure used to deliver Deskwork may receive standard technical information associated with a web request, such as an IP address, browser information, device information, requested pages, and timestamps.

This information may be processed by the hosting and infrastructure providers involved in delivering the website.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Deskwork may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality or where third-party services are enabled.

At the time of this policy's publication, Deskwork is not claiming that Google AdSense advertising is already active on the site.

5. Advertising

Deskwork may use advertising services in the future to help support the continued development and hosting of the site.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant advertising technologies, cookies, data processing, and available privacy choices before or when those services are used.

6. Third-party services

Deskwork may rely on third-party infrastructure, hosting, analytics, advertising, or other services where needed to operate and improve the website.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. External links

Deskwork may link to external websites and services. Deskwork is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites.

8. Children's privacy

Deskwork is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and the site does not knowingly request personal information from children for account creation.

9. Security

Deskwork aims to use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the website and its services. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Deskwork's tools, services, infrastructure, or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information provided on the Deskwork website.

This Privacy Policy is provided for general informational purposes and is not legal advice.

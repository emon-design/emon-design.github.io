Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Deskwork.

The tools themselves

The resume builder, image compressor/converter, GPA calculator, and citation generator all run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or upload into these tools — your resume content, images, grades, or citation details — is sent to or stored on any server. If you close the tab without downloading your result, it's gone.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and you can opt out of personalized advertising through your Google Ad Settings.

Cookies

Ad partners may set cookies to control ad frequency and measure ad performance. This site does not itself set cookies to track you across other sites.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics (such as visit counts and which tools are used) to understand which tools are helpful and improve the site. This data is aggregate and not tied to your identity.

Premium purchases

If you purchase premium features, payment is handled by a third-party payment processor (such as Gumroad). We do not receive or store your card details — that information is handled entirely by the payment provider under their own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Deskwork is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
